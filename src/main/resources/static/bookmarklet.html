Getting LinkedIn, Naukri and Instahyre in

None of the three publishes a job-search API, and automating their sites is against their terms — so the engine does not. This is the honest way in: you are reading a posting in your own browser, and this button hands it to the engine in one click instead of a select-all-copy-paste.

Drag this to your bookmarks bar:

📥 Send to Job Hunt

Then: open any job posting, click the bookmark. The engine opens with the posting staged, company and title already read out of it. You check the three boxes and press Add — same as any paste, minus the copying.

What it does

and what it does not
  • Reads the text of the page you are looking at, plus its URL and title.
  • Submits them to 127.0.0.1:6969 as an ordinary form post — no background requests, no permissions, nothing installed.
  • Does not log in anywhere, follow links, read other pages, or run unless you click it.
  • Nothing is saved until you press Add in the engine.
